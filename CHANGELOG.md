### Post-Release Erratum (`v0.15.0` Audit Findings — Remediation Candidates on `main`, In Review for Upcoming `v0.16.0`)

A post-release audit of tag `v0.15.0` identified six accuracy, memory-safety, state-isolation, and tour-provenance defects in the `v0.15.0` release artifacts (including that 2D water suppression, IPAP `2D CON`/`CACO`, and water-suppression undo were shipped as done in `v0.15.0` without external review); remediation candidates landed on `main` (**In Review — awaiting Claude FINAL APPROVE** for upcoming `v0.16.0`):

1. **Raw Bruker IPAP `2D CON` / `CACO` Trace Count & Spin-State Recombination (`v0150:P0-1`, `P1-4`)**: Shipped in `v0.15.0` without external review, `recombine_ipap_2d` multiplied `acqu2s.TD` by an extra factor of `2` (treating `TD1` as complex-pair count rather than stored scalar `L[0]=2 * FnMODE=2` traces), hardcoded $J_{\text{C}'\text{C}\alpha} = 53\text{ Hz}$, `ap_scale = 1.0`, and `IP`-first `L[0]` order, and fell back to `0..200 ppm` indirect axes when `proc2s` was absent—rendering real Bruker TopSpin `c_hcacon_ia` / `c_hccaco_ia` reconstructions **broken** (`r = 0.009` vs TopSpin `splitcomb ipap2`). Remediation candidate on `main` (`c10a47f`, `37da994`) with `td1_stored / 2` loop count, `pulprog` `cnst`/delay $J$ & `L[0]` `IP`/`AP` order parsing, complex scalar `ap_scale` fitting, blind `SF`/`OFFSET` referencing (`r = 0.999997–1.000000` `[in-sample]` across `6` isolated Ubiquitin pairs), and fail-closed unsplit raw 3D IPAP rejection.
2. **Hostile Binary Header & Archive Decompression Pre-Allocation Caps (`v0150:P0-2`, `P1-7`, `P1-8`, `P1-9`)**: `v0.15.0` contained unguarded `usize` dimension products and pre-allocation paths on crafted Bruker `acqus`/`procs`, NMRPipe `.ft2`/`.ft3`, Sparky `.ucsf`, and browser `.tgz`/`.zip` archives. Remediation candidate on `main` (`c5ccaaf`, `c10a47f`) via `checked_mul` overflow guards, pre-allocation voxel/byte caps validated against on-disk payload lengths, and `256 MiB` streaming archive decompression ceilings (`spectrum_hostile_tests` `32/32`).
3. **Post-FFT 2D `F1` Water-Suppression Solute Attenuation (`v0150:P1-5`)**: Shipped in `v0.15.0` without external review, applying post-FFT 2D Marion–Ikura–Bax water suppression filtered both `F2` and `F1` unconditionally, attenuating `HN–Hα` / heteronuclear cross-peaks near `(8.3, 4.50..4.65 ppm)` by `6.6%–28%`. Remediation candidate on `main` (`c10a47f`, `37da994`) by defaulting `WaterSuppress2dConfig::suppress_homonuclear_f1 = false` (`> 98–99%` `[synthetic self-consistency]` solute peak retention while suppressing the `F2` water ridge) and requiring genuine solvent-peak dominance before 1D auto-detection suppresses solute peaks.
4. **Single-Level `Option` Water-Suppression Undo & 2D-to-3D Cube Mutation (`v0150:P1-6`)**: Shipped in `v0.15.0` without external review, `ws` stored a 1-level `Option` undo slot (`ws` twice overwrote the original spectrum) and 2D `ws` mutated loaded 3D cubes (`hncacb_3d`/`hnco_3d`). Remediation candidate on `main` (`0e01472`, `e3f208a`) via bounded multi-level `KeyedSnapshotStack` (`ws, ws, wu, wu` lossless across 1D/2D/3D) and `Arc<Spectrum3D>` isolation inside the unified monotonic User+MCP `UndoJournal`.
5. **Divergent 1D Solvent Referencing & Solute Multiplet Snapping (`v0150:P1-3`)**: In `v0.15.0`, CLI and GUI 1D solvent auto-referencing used divergent heuristics that could snap onto high-SNR solute multiplets near residual solvent windows. Remediation candidate on `main` (`b32d11f`) via unified `process_loaded_1d` singlet/linewidth/amplitude safety guards and first-principles IUPAC $\Xi$ `-2.665 ppm` `13C` DSS-vs-TMS offset (`--c13-ref dss|tms`).
6. **Interactive Tour Modules `04`–`08` Schematic `<canvas>` Curves, Silent Fallbacks & Demo Candor (`v0150:P1-1`, `P1-2`, `P2`)**: In `v0.15.0`, `build_interactive_tours.py` rendered analytical JS schematics on Modules `04`–`08` while `.tour.json` exported real engine `payload` arrays (causing `tau_m = 8.1 ns` caption drift vs `4.08 ns` [`4.0837 ns`] in `payload`), permitted silent schematic fallbacks on Modules `01`–`03`, used `.innerHTML` on tooltips, omitted the disclosures that Tour `HNCACB` strip markers are reference chemical shifts rather than picked peaks and that non-constant-time Wittekind–Mueller `HNCACB` (`2*d28 = 7.2 ms`) yields `Gly10` `CA (+1)`, and dropped `"Synthetic Ubiquitin Demo Suite"` from `docs/demo/index.html`.
