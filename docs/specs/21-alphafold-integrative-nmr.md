### 1.2 Clean-Room Kabsch–Sander (1983) `8 -> 4`-State Secondary-Structure Classifier (`nmr-core::kabsch_sander_ss`)

1. **Amide Proton Reconstruction & Partial-Charge Electrostatic H-Bond Energy**:
   - Reconstructs missing *trans*-peptide amide protons $\mathbf{r}_{\text{H}_j} = \mathbf{r}_{\text{N}_j} + 1.00\text{ \AA}\cdot\widehat{(\mathbf{r}_{\text{C}'_{j-1}} - \mathbf{r}_{\text{O}_{j-1}})}$ when $d(\text{C}'_{j-1}, \text{N}_j) \le 2.5\text{ \AA}$ and evaluates Kabsch–Sander electrostatic interaction energy ($q_1 = 0.42, q_2 = 0.20$, `r_min >= 0.5 Å` clash floor) for $|i - j| \ge 2$:
     $$E_{\text{HB}}(i \to j) = 332\,q_1 q_2 \left(\frac{1}{r_{\text{O}_i\text{N}_j}} + \frac{1}{r_{\text{C}'_i\text{H}_j}} - \frac{1}{r_{\text{O}_i\text{H}_j}} - \frac{1}{r_{\text{C}'_i\text{N}_j}}\right)\text{ kcal mol}^{-1}$$
     retaining the two strongest H-bonds per donor and acceptor satisfying $E_{\text{HB}} < -0.5\text{ kcal mol}^{-1}$.
2. **Canonical 8-State Precedence & Deterministic `8 -> 4`-State NMR Reduction**:
   - Enforces the exact Kabsch & Sander (*Biopolymers* **1983**, 22, 2577) summary precedence **`H > E > B > G > I > T > S > C`** so extended $\beta$-ladders (`E`) strictly outrank isolated $\beta$-bridges (`B`), and demotes any orphan `< 3`-residue `G` ($3_{10}$) or `< 5`-residue `I` ($\pi$) fragment remaining after higher-priority overwrites to H-bonded turn `T`.
   - Maps `KabschSanderState8` + $(\phi_i, \psi_i)$ into `SecondaryStructure4`: **`Helix` (`H`)** `{H, G, I}`, **`Sheet` (`E`)** `{E, B}`, **`Ppii` (`P`)** non-H-bonded `{T, S, C}` in $\phi \in [-105^\circ, -45^\circ], \psi \in [+115^\circ, +175^\circ]$ (Adzhubei et al. **2013**), and **`Coil` (`C`)** remaining `{T, S, C}`.

### 1.3 Original `pLDDT` vs `RCI-S²` / Lipari–Szabo $S^2$ Concordance & 6-Class Gap-Free Taxonomy

1. **Rigidity Source Distinction & Berjanskii–Wishart (`2005`) `RCI-S²` Scaling**:
   - Explicitly records `RigiditySource::ExperimentalRelaxationS2` when Lipari–Szabo $S^2_i$ (`--s2 <modelfree.tsv>`) is supplied vs `RigiditySource::ShiftDerivedRci` (**`RCI-S²`**, never labelled `"experimental S²"`) when derived from chemical shifts.
   - Uses the canonical Berjanskii & Wishart (*JACS* **2005**, 127, 14970) `RCI` scaling constant **`17.7`** (`RCI_i <= 0.175` ceiling) and applies `[0.25, 0.50, 0.25]` 3-point smoothing **strictly across contiguous assigned neighbors** (`seq_id` step `1`), never averaging across unassigned sequence gaps.
2. **Original Rigidity Mapping, Ordered-Core Correlation & Block-Bootstrap 95% CIs**:
   - Maps $\text{pLDDT}_i \in [0, 100]$ via FERRIC's original heuristic $\hat{S}^2_{\text{AF},i} = \operatorname{clamp}\!\bigl((\text{pLDDT}_i - 35.0) / 55.0,\; 0.0,\; 1.0\bigr)$ and reports:
     - `spearman_rho: Option<f64>` (`None` rather than `0.0` whenever either vector has zero variance, e.g. constant `pLDDT`),
     - `spearman_rho_ordered: Option<f64>` restricted to predicted-ordered core residues ($\text{pLDDT}_i \ge 70.0$) so terminal disorder contrast does not inflate global $\rho_s$,
     - `spearman_ci95` and `rmsd_rig_ci95` via deterministic 500-replicate moving-block bootstrap (block length `5` residues).
3. **6-Class Gap-Free Per-Residue Concordance Taxonomy (`AfNmrConcordanceClass`)**:
   Every $(\text{pLDDT}, S^2)$ region maps to exactly one class. The thresholds are inline literals in `evaluate_af_nmr_concordance` (`pLDDT` `70.0`; $S^2$ `0.60`, `0.65`, `0.72`), not named constants, and the borderline bands are reported as `Indeterminate`:
   - **`ConcordantOrdered`**: $\text{pLDDT}_i \ge 70.0$, $S^2_i \ge 0.65$, no `Helix <-> Sheet` flip, and circular dihedral divergence $\Delta\theta_i < 60^\circ$ (when `TorsionDb` confidence is `Good`).
   - **`ConcordantDisordered`**: $\text{pLDDT}_i < 70.0$ and $S^2_i < 0.65$.
   - **`ModelOrderedSolutionDisordered`** *(serde alias `ConditionalFoldingOrCrystalArtifact`)*: $\text{pLDDT}_i \ge 70.0$, $S^2_i < 0.60$, AF `Helix`/`Sheet`, and NMR $p_{\text{coil}} + p_{\text{PPII}} \ge 0.65$ (high-confidence model helix/sheet that is dynamically disordered in solution, e.g. conditionally folding $\alpha$-MoRFs).
   - **`DynamicHingeOrAltState`**: Either ($\text{pLDDT}_i < 70.0$ and $S^2_i \ge 0.72$) or ($\text{pLDDT}_i \ge 70.0$, $S^2_i \ge 0.65$, and `Helix <-> Sheet` flip or $\Delta\theta_i \ge 60^\circ$).
   - **`Indeterminate`**: Borderline bands where the thresholds do not separate ordered from disordered: $\text{pLDDT}_i \ge 70$ with $0.60 \le S^2_i < 0.65$; $\text{pLDDT}_i \ge 70$ with $S^2_i < 0.60$ that does not meet the `ModelOrderedSolutionDisordered` condition; $\text{pLDDT}_i < 70$ with $0.65 \le S^2_i < 0.72$. ### 4.2 Convex Per-Nucleus Log-Linear Replacement Algebra (Zero Double-Counting)

Let `seq_offsets` be the single unlabelled reference offset vector estimated by `estimate_reference_offsets` (`0` separate `af_shift_offsets` gauge drift), and let **`[dev-set]`** `AF_SIGMA_DEV_SET_PPM` (`H: 0.52, N: 2.80, CA: 1.05, CB: 1.20, C: 1.05 ppm`, `T21.2` dev-set LOPO RMSE floor; aliased as `AF_SIGMA_OOS_PPM` for backwards compatibility) floor the `T21.2` per-residue predictive uncertainty $\sigma^{\text{eff}}_{k,a} = \max(\hat{\sigma}_{k,a}, \sigma^{\text{dev}}_a)$. For each observed nucleus $a \in \mathcal{O}_s$, `af_assign_priors` evaluates both the sequence-prior log-odds $\ell_{\text{seq}}(s, k, a)$ and the clamped Student-$t(\nu = 4)$ AF log-odds $\ell_{\text{AF}}(s, k, a)$ against the **identical** `Priors::background` null density $\ell_{\text{bg},a}$, and forms a **convex replacement** weighted by $\omega_r = \operatorname{clamp}\!\bigl(\lambda_{\text{AF}}\,w_{\text{AF}}(\text{pLDDT}_k),\; 0.0,\; 1.0\bigr)$:

$$U^{\text{AF}}_{s,k} = \left[\sum_{a \in \mathcal{O}_s} \Bigl((1 - \omega_r)\,\ell_{\text{seq}}(s, k, a) + \omega_r\,\operatorname{clamp}\bigl(\ell_{t_4}(z^{\text{AF}}_{s,k,a}) - \ell_{\text{bg},a},\; -U_{\max}^{\text{clamp}}, +U_{\max}^{\text{clamp}}\bigr)\Bigr)\right] - \ln(n_{\text{pos}})$$

- When $\omega_r = 0$ (`--af-weight 0` or `pLDDT -> 0`), $U^{\text{AF}}_{s,k} \equiv U^{\text{seq}}_{s,k}$ identically.
- When $\omega_r = 1$, the AF likelihood cleanly **replaces** the sequence BMRB Gaussian for that nucleus rather than double-counting `AA_k` identity, applying `-ln(n_pos)` exactly once per spin system.
- **Scope-Limit Note on Sparse `HNCA`-Only Loop Errors**: On full 7-spectrum triple-resonance data, corrupting a 12-residue loop (`res 40..52`) by $120^\circ$ at overconfident `pLDDT = 95` causes `0` accuracy loss (`349/360 -> 352/360` `standard`, `349/360 -> 350/360` `evo-graph` `[synthetic self-consistency]`), whereas on sparse `HNCA`-only data overall accuracy rises (`6.82% -> 13.35%`, `24/352 -> 47/352`, HC `0/0 -> 3/4`) while local inside-loop (`40..52`) accuracy drops (`7/60 [11.67%] -> 3/60 [5.00%]` `[synthetic self-consistency]`); users on sparse `HNCA`-only datasets should inspect suspect loops via `ferric af-validate`.

### 7.1 Discrete-Scan Marginalized `BIC`, Experimental-Noise `H0` & Scope Limits (`nmr-core::excited_state`)

1. **Input Integrity & Distinct-Residue Floor (`MIN_COMMITTED_OBSERVATIONS = 4`)**:
   - Rejects duplicate `(chain_id, seq_id, atom_name)` rows across single or multiple `--exchange` files (`Err`, preventing $2\ln\mathrm{BF}$ inflation by repeating a file) and requires $\ge 4$ **distinct residues** and **distinct exchanging residues** (`cfg.min_obs_to_commit = MIN_COMMITTED_OBSERVATIONS = 4`, checked against both `n_distinct_residues` and `n_distinct_exchanging_residues`) before evaluating `Committed` verdicts (4 nuclei on 1 residue abstain as `AmbiguousOrUnmodeledState`).
   - Tracks `# cpmg_prefiltered_significant_only` (`ExcitedStateConfig::cpmg_prefiltered_significant_only`) explicitly in input headers and output reports so pre-filtered significant `CPMG` dispersions are distinguished from unfiltered rows.
2. **Discrete-Scan Marginalized Likelihood & `BIC` (`n = n_distinct_residues`, `default_k()`)**:
   - Evaluates the uniform-prior **marginalized log-likelihood** over each discrete candidate set $M_H \ge 1$ via log-sum-exp, combined with family parameter counts `ExcitedStateHypothesisKind::default_k()` ($k_{H_0} = 0$, $k_{H_1} = 2$, $k_{H_2} = 1$, $k_{H_3} = 2$, $k_{H_4} = 1$) and $n = n_{\text{distinct\_residues}}$:
     $$\ln\bar{L}_H = \ln\!\left(\frac{1}{M_H}\sum_{m=1}^{M_H} \exp\bigl(\ell(H, m)\bigr)\right), \qquad \mathrm{BIC}(H) = -2\ln\bar{L}_H + k_H\ln n_{\text{distinct\_residues}}$$
     naturally penalizing large discrete hypothesis spaces ($-\ln M_H$ Occam factor, invariant to appending duplicate `--alt-structures` conformers).
3. **Experimental-Noise-Only Null `H0`, `UnmodeledSignificantExchange`, `BF_12` & Error-Scale Diagnostic (`Chi2ConsistencyDiagnostic`)**:
   - Evaluates `H0` ($\Delta\varpi_{AB} = 0$) strictly against experimental measurement uncertainty $\sigma_{\text{exp},i,a}$ (`0` forward-model $\sigma_{\text{fwd}}$ inflation on `H0`). When `H0` is strongly rejected ($2\ln\mathrm{BF}_{\text{best},H_0} \ge 10$ or $\chi^2_{\nu,\text{exp}}(H_0) > 4.0$) yet every physical candidate $H_1..H_4$ fits poorly ($\chi^2_\nu(H_{\text{best}}) > 2.5$), `classify_excited_state` emits `ExcitedStateVerdict::AmbiguousOrUnmodeledState` with `abstention_kind = Some(ExcitedStateAbstentionKind::UnmodeledSignificantExchange)` rather than letting `H0` win or force-committing a wrong mechanism.
   - Reports `two_ln_bf_top1_vs_top2 = hyps[1].bic - hyps[0].bic` (comparing rank-1 against rank-2 `hyps[1]`, which can be `H0`) separately from `two_ln_bf_top1_vs_runnerup_non_null` (comparing rank-1 against the best runner-up non-null family `H1..H4`, requiring $\ge 6.0$ to commit).
   - Populates `chi2_consistency_diagnostic: Option<Chi2ConsistencyDiagnostic>` and `error_scale_warning: Option<String>` across core, CLI, and MCP. **`FIX3` `21F:B19` (In Review — awaiting Claude FINAL APPROVE)**: the warning no longer fires on `H0` rejection or `UnmodeledSignificantExchange` alone; it requires evidence of mis-specified errors, tested against the exact $\chi^2$ quantile (`chi2_upper_quantile`, regularized incomplete gamma; `ERROR_SCALE_CHI2_CONFIDENCE = 0.99`) rather than a normal approximation. Evidence, in order: (1) control sites (new exchange-TSV column `site_role = exchange|control`; controls have expected $\Delta\varpi = 0$) with $\chi^2/n > \chi^2_{0.99,n}/n$; else (2) spectator sites of the best model (predicted no shift, $\sigma_{\text{tot}} \le 1.05\,\sigma_{\Delta\varpi}$, robust clip $|z| > 3.5\max(1, s_{\text{MAD}})$ = `ERROR_SCALE_OUTLIER_ABS_Z`, at least `MIN_ERROR_SCALE_SPECTATOR_SITES = 5`) with $\chi^2_\nu > \chi^2_{0.99,\nu}/\nu$; else (3) the best model's $\chi^2_\nu$ with $\nu = n_{\text{obs}} - k$. When it fires, committed, `UnmodeledSignificantExchange` and `ConsistentWithNoise` outcomes are downgraded to the new abstention kind `ExcitedStateAbstentionKind::ErrorScaleInconsistent`. New API: `classify_excited_state_with_controls`. Unsupported exchange nuclei (outside `SUPPORTED_EXCHANGE_ATOMS`) fail closed with `UNSUPPORTED_EXCHANGE_ATOM_MESSAGE`. `test_fix3_b19_error_scale_warning_informative_and_blocks_false_calls` asserts `>= 90%` per case over its seed sweep (default `10`, `FERRIC_B19_SEEDS`): true `H1` with correct errors and `H0` with correct errors do not warn; `H0` with errors under-stated `2×`/`3×` warns and makes no confident call.
4. **Corrected Pelton (`1993`) `His` `CG` Sign, Sidechain-`15N` Eligibility & Unsigned-`CPMG` Direction Guard**:
   - Corrects `His` $\mathrm{N}^{\delta 1}\text{–H} \to \mathrm{N}^{\varepsilon 2}\text{–H}$ differential shifts to Pelton et al. (*Protein Sci.* **1993**, 2, 543, Table 1): $\Delta\delta_{\text{CG}} = \mathbf{+8.3\text{ ppm}}$ (`127.8 -> 136.1 ppm`), $\Delta\delta_{\text{CD2}} = -7.2\text{ ppm}$ (`124.2 -> 117.0 ppm`), $\Delta\delta_{\text{ND1}} \approx -82\text{ ppm}$, $\Delta\delta_{\text{NE2}} \approx +82\text{ ppm}$; enables `His` tautomer/protonation sub-hypotheses whenever sidechain `ND1`/`NE2` rows are present even without `CD2`/`CG`; and returns `HisDirectionality::AmbiguousDirectionOnUnsignedCpmg` when all `His`-proximal rows are unsigned `CPMG` magnitudes.
5. **Explicit Biophysical Scope Limit (Endpoint-Only $\Delta\varpi_{AB}$)**:
   - `nmr-core::excited_state` scores pure 2-state chemical-shift endpoint differences $\Delta\varpi_{AB} = \varpi_B - \varpi_A$ (`H1` full local coil `[u, v]`, `H2` full *cis/trans* switch, `H3` discrete rotamer/tautomer state, `H4` supplied 3D conformer). It fits **zero** fractional partial-unfolding amplitude $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ and **zero** excited-state population $p_B$ or exchange rate $k_{\text{ex}}$ (which are fitted upstream by `nmr-core::{cest, cpmg}`); partial helix fraying or unmodeled multi-state intermediates surface honestly as `AmbiguousOrUnmodeledState` (`abstention_kind = UnmodeledSignificantExchange` when `H0` is rejected).
